Hui-Ling Zhen

AI for Verification | AI for EDA | LLM Reasoning | Self-evolve Agent

I obtained my Ph.D. from the Department of Science, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2017. I work on agent algorithms and their industrial applications in formal verification and testing, with extensive experience in LLM reasoning, long-context training, post-training, agent self-evolution, and large-scale optimization.

I am particularly interested in agent algorithms and their applications to complex optimization problems, such as building agents with agents to solve industrial EDA formal verification problems. I have a sustained passion for self-evolving agents.

Research Highlights

AI/LLM for EDA

AI-augmented SAT solving, data augmentation, and LLM-driven modeling for ATPG, LEC, model checking, testbench generation, and bug finding.

Industrial Agents

Production agent systems with tool invocation, memory, collaborative reasoning, execution-time recovery, verifier feedback, and continuous improvement loops.

Agentic RL

Post-training for reasoning and tool-using agents, including reward shaping, rollout optimization, dense supervision, and training-inference co-design.

Selected Projects

LLM and Agents for Industrial EDA

Developed industrially viable AI-driven SAT solving and data augmentation methods for formal verification and testing.

Applications include ATPG, LEC, bug finding, model checking, and Verilog generation; deployed in real chip verification scenarios.

Industrial-Grade Agent Deployment

Built and maintained agent systems for formal verification, bug finding, code review, and deep research.

Achieved up to 8x end-to-end speedup in deep research agents while reducing interaction rounds and tool calls.

Agentic RL and Co-design

Designed post-training pipelines for large reasoning and agentic models under fixed compute budgets.

Improved long-context and agent benchmarks by up to 20%; explored quantization and speculative decoding for efficient training and inference.
